# DrivePass UK

Prepare for the UK driving theory test with bilingual revision in English and Arabic.

Features:
- English and Arabic questions
- Topic practice
- Timed mock tests
- Explanations
- Bookmarks and wrong-answer review
- Progress tracking
- Searchable traffic-sign guide
- English and Arabic voice reading
- Offline revision

DrivePass UK is an independent revision application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DVSA.
