# DrivePass UK Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

DrivePass UK is an independent driving-theory revision application.

## Data processed

The current offline version stores practice history, bookmarks and application settings locally on the user's device. The application does not require an account and does not send this information to the developer.

## Permissions

The application does not request access to contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone or camera for its core functions.

## Third-party services

Production builds may use Expo and the distribution services provided by Apple and Google. Their own privacy terms apply to store downloads and platform services.

## Children

The application is intended as revision support for people preparing for the UK driving theory test. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.
